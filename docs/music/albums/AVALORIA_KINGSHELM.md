# Avaloria — Volume I: Kingshelm City

Status: **Album proposed** | 2026-11-02

## Narrative and identity

The VR story opens in the prosperous Southern Province capital of Kingshelm, ruled by King Valius Knightsfield. The player arrives at its lively harbor, is suddenly detained because of the kingdom's institutional fear of the Void, escapes imprisonment, explores the city, joins the Adventurer's Guild, learns of the Royal Academy and eventually leaves the city for adventure.

**Narrative tension:** An inspiring, beautiful and socially alive fantasy kingdom can also be fearful and unjust. Preserve the beauty of the city even when scoring the cruelty of its institutions.

**Musical thesis:** Welcoming, human-scaled orchestral high fantasy, with a recognizable motif that can become ceremonial, fractured, intimate or adventurous depending on the district and story beat.

## Album-level production requirements

- Do not mistake generic folk dance for everyday medieval-fantasy atmosphere.
- Keep environmental harbor/market/crowd effects in spatial game audio where possible.
- Treat the harborside arrival's safety and the arrest's shock as **separate** audio jobs; the normal harbor loop remains welcoming.
- Develop a compact Kingshelm motif in Track 01, then let it change shape in royalty, prison, slums and departure; actual motif must be identified after audio exists.
- City/district cues should support long VR visits and restrained looping.
- Narrative triggers and musical stings are separate additions to the ambient album.

## Workflow

1. Read/update each location's lore and place in the VR story.
2. Finalize its track brief against the album identity.
3. Draft Suno style (<=1,000 characters) and arrangement (<=5,000 characters).
4. Generate, listen, compare, revise and record.
5. Register successful motifs and each approval decision.
6. Test loops/transitions in the game before marking Implemented.
