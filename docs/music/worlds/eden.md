# Eden — Music identity

**Status:** Initial alignment; not a completed canonical music specification.

## World and culture
Alien hyper-jungle, tribes living within the jungle and buildings, ancient biological life.

## Provisional sound palette
Biological percussion, thumb piano/marimba/plucked materials, rich polyrhythms, organic-electronic sound design.

## Albums / subregions to plan
Canopy settlements, jungle floor, ancient ruins, tribal communities.

## Decisions / guardrails
Avoid conventional 'generic jungle' scoring and invented military-base cultures. Separate ambient ecological score from celebratory community music.

## Workflow
Research the original lore and VR route for each locale. Complete an album sheet with the [template](../templates/ALBUM.md). Approve the album's musical voice through real listening before defining motifs as established. Use the [track template](../templates/TRACK.md) for each song. Keep regional decisions here when they affect more than one album.
