# Avaloria — Music identity

**Status:** Initial alignment; not a completed canonical music specification.

## World and culture
Fantasy cultures, ancient living landscapes, kingdoms, adventure and discovery.

## Provisional sound palette
Orchestral acoustic foundation, culturally specific palettes, shared motifs interpreted differently by each society.

## Albums / subregions to plan
Kingshelm, Wildkin, Highborne, Frost Elves, Stone-Root, Deeprock, Salt-Beard, Primal Isles.

## Decisions / guardrails
Never collapse the disparate dwarven cultures into one generic palette. Keep exploration restrained and departure distinct. Follow the VR narrative as the initial composition route.

## Workflow
Research the original lore and VR route for each locale. Complete an album sheet with the [template](../templates/ALBUM.md). Approve the album's musical voice through real listening before defining motifs as established. Use the [track template](../templates/TRACK.md) for each song. Keep regional decisions here when they affect more than one album.
