# Music decisions — Cross-project registry

Only **global** or **cross-world** decisions belong here. World-level and album-level decisions belong in their respective sheets.

| Date | Scope | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2026-11-02 | Global | Judge generated audio by listening; prompts alone cannot be approved recordings | Keep creative and implementation statuses honest |
